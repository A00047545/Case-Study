## Historical development

### Home Radio (1920):
##### The first major device to enter ordinary people homes was the radio. commercial radio broadcasting started in the early 1920 following the research of Guglielmo Marconi (most credited) and further advanced by Lee de Forest, inventor of Audition vacuum tube amplifier (1906). By 1920 stations like KDKA in Pittsburgh began radio stations, radio became the first mass-media technology, influencing politics, bringing news, music and entertainment in general for the population directly to their homes. Fun fact about radio: Early development of radio was done by Nikola Tesla himself, making people think he was the inventor of radio.

### Television (1930-1950):
##### Television was initially part of experiments in mechanical scanning led by John Logie Baird (1925) and became a fully electronic system developed by Philo Farnsworth in 1927. Even though the television was invented before, the popularization only occurred after the second world war when affordable cathode ray tube televisions started being sold. Aswell as the radio, television also reshaped communication, politics, advertisement and it is still to this day considered one of the biggest pieces of consumer technology.

### Transistor (1947):
##### Even though it wasn’t directly used by the consumers like a television or a radio, transistors played a crucial role in the evolution of consumer technology and the world of electronic devices in general. Invented in 1947 by John Bardeen, Walter Brattain and Willian Shockley, the transistor substituted the big and bulky vacuum tubes with a reliable portable device. Because of that, electronic devices became more compact, and transistors became the foundation for devices such as computers and all modern devices as such.

### Home Appliances (1950-1960):
##### After World War II, industrial expansion of household appliances such as refrigerators, vacuum cleaners, microwaves and home electronics in general became very popular due to its now accessible prices. The reduction of prices came to be due to advances in electrical engineering (such as the invention of the transistor) and mass production, drastically improving food safety (with refrigerators), hygiene and domestic labour.

### Early video games (1960-1970):
##### The first video game in history is generally considered to be “Tennis for two”, a two-player game in which the goal is to hit a ball back and forth, whoever miss the ball loses the game. Tennis for two was developed in research institutes in 1958 by the physicist William Higinbotham, but it wasn’t until Atari’s “Pong” designed by Allan Alcorn under Nolan Bushnell’s direction that video games became a commercial success. The success Digital entertainment in the form of video games led to the development of arcade machines and first-generation home-consoles.

### Birth of personal computer (1970-1980):
##### The first computer in history ENIAC (Electronic Numerical Integrator and Computer) was invented in 1945, but it was a massive amalgamation of cables and connectors, covering 1800 square feet, but it wasn’t until Altair 8800 invented in 1975 that inspired Bill Gates and Paul Allen to create devices like Apple II, in 1977, the IBM PC (1981) and Macintosh (1984) which established a user friendly interface and set a standard for hardware in general. Because of them, computers became accessible to homes, schools, and business.

### Spread of the internet and world wide web(1990s)
##### Marked by fast technological advancements and the emergence of friendly tools that enabled people to navigate on the web, the world wide web was invented in 1989-1991 by Tim Berners-Lee. The world wide web enabled people to communicate and share information across the globe. Around 1993 and 1994 browsers like mosaic and Netscape navigators made it accessible to consumers, reshaping communication, business, education, entertainment, etc. 

### Mobile phones (1990-2000)
##### Mobile phones were introduced in 1972 with early radio telephones, by the American engineer Martin Cooper, known as the father of cellular phone, He made the first mobile phone call using the prototype Motorola DynaTAC 8000X. With 2g digital networks, they became smaller, their prices dropped and they also became more available worldwide. Brands like Motorola and Nokia were the main companies to popularize mobile communications and mobile internet, they set a foundation for the development of smartphones.

### Smartphones (2007- today)
##### Apple’s iPhone, led by the American businessman, investor and inventor Steve Jobs introduced in 2007 pocket-sized phones with the capability of browsing the internet, allowed the use of apps within the phone and multimedia. While apple initiated the smartphone advancements, the widespread adoption of smartphones by the Android operating system launched by google in 2008. Android’s operating model allowed companies such as Samsung to produce affordable and customizable devices, therefore becoming increasingly popular. By 2018 80% of smartphone sales worldwide were Android-powered devices.

### AI Integration (2015- today)
##### Amazon Echo (2014) and Google home (2016) popularized AI in daily life, before these devices AI were an abstract concept used in labs and specialized companies, but these devices made AI part of consumers daily life in form of speakers. Voice assistants such as Alexa and google assistant made talking to machines feel natural since now with its advancements, interactions including asking for the temperature in your city, turn the lights in the room on and off (hands-free) and causal interactions such as asking for a joke. automated homes with energy efficient and interconnected services are the main impacts of AI integration for consumers.
