


## 1. Historical Evolution: From Origins to the Present 

### a. Evolution of Social Media Platforms 

 
 
##### Social media began in the early 2000s, with MySpace being the first media site to reach 1 million users per month, and it reached its peak monthly users around 2004. MySpace was a simple platform that allowed users to build personalized profiles, upload music and backgrounds, and connect with friends. We  can safely say that it paved the way for the next generations of platforms such as Facebook and instagram.  These new platforms allow for better privacy settings, offered a cleaner design and faster communication between servers to allow and all in general faster app. As apps platforms developed, some have shifted toward more of a short quick pace video format. Apps such as Vine, TikTok, and instagram are a good example, because of this, people got addictive due to the dopamine they gave us.

### b. Development of Search Engines 
 
##### The early stages of search engines were very  simple, with Yahoo (founded in 1994) relying on manual listing, which had users manually search and browse pre-made categories. In 1988, Google was launched, which revolutionized web browsing with its new PageRank  algorithm , which is still used today. This algorithm is based on ranking websites based on relevance and links. This allowed Google to be faster, more user friendly,  and quickly gain users, beating their competitors. In todays world, the search engines were introduced to the  so called “AI-powerd era” with tools like chat-gpt, Google Gemini, and Microsoft Copoilot. This not only helped with the speed and the accuracy of the users search but also tries to understand the users intent and provide an answer.

### c. Transformation of Streaming Services 

##### The early stages of streaming services started in the late 1990s and early  2000s starting out as physical media such as VHS tapes, DVDs, and CDs. The early 2000s saw the rise of streaming services such as Netflix which went from a DVD-by-mail service into an online powerhouse in 2007, offering downloadable films and tv shows on demand. For music, streaming services like, sound cloud and  spotify quickly rose in popularity allowing for music to be streamed legally instead of downloading them. By 2010s, streaming services had become mainstream with companies such as Disney+, Amazone Prime Video, and Hulu capitalized on the growth for the demand for streaming content.

### d. Development of Instant Messaging  
 
##### Instant messaging became a thing in the late 80s, with the first publicly available instant messenger, which was the IRC (Internet Relay Chat), and it spread globally quickly in 1989. This was taking place in so called “chat-rooms” with real time online conversations. Instant messeging truly became popular when BBM was introduced to Blackberry devices inn 2005. The 2000s had a major breakthrough with apps like Skype which introduced free voice and video calling, Whatsapp(2009) that allowed to chat over the internet instead of SMS, Discord(2015) expanded the idea of calling and texting and included things like communities and channels. This concept was greatly used by gamers and groups as it was free, easy to use, and offerd more then aything at its time for its targeted audiance.

## 2. Business and Societal Viewpoints 

### a. Business Perspective 

##### From a business perspective, the technological progress has allowed for companies to now advertise their products easier then before. Social media became a main source for a lot of companies, with some reaching an average revenue up to 40% by just advertising online. Companies can also use personalized advertising towards its users by collecting data, which is a smart way of getting sales. An example of that would be TikToks algorithm helps brands to gain user data and lower costs compared to traditional advertising,Streaming services also changed from one time payments to subscriptions, which allows the company to track their income more accurately. 

### b. Societal Perspective 
 
##### From a societal viewpoint, the evolution of technologies of the internet has brought people together all across the world but also influemced a lot off things. For exmaple, social media has influenced the fashion and food trends, world news and local events, political and community events. Social media also created a new type of marketing. On the other hand, this has also raised online concerns of privacy, misinformation being spread, and the mental health effects of the constant engagement people have. Looking into the future things like AR(augmented reality) and VR(virtual reality) will have a massive influence on how people engage online. Not only that but also AI and machine learning will also shape the way the users experience, to a more in depth personalized experience.

 
## 3. Ethical Implications and Proposed Improvements 

##### The rapid evolution of consumer technology has created many ethical issues. First, there is a risk of privacy and data protection. A lot of platforms and recommender systems collect and store your data without telling you to give you more personolized ads and recommendations. And while this can improve our expeirence, it should also raise our concerns and question if this is ethically the right thing to do. Secondly, AI powered searches can give us algorithmic bias and unfairness can lead to discrimination, if AI systems are trained on a small database it can lead to it favouring certain groups or content types, or apply harmful stereotypes.

## 4. Conclusion 

##### So in summary, the evolution of consumer technology from social media that went from MySpace to TikTok, Search Engines that evolved from Yahoo to AI-Powered searches, streaming services that went from simple DVDs to Netflix and instant messaging that became an everyday thing for most people. Each breakthrough has changed the way we live our daily life, from communication, entertainment, and sharing information. From a business viewpoint, the evolution has created new industries and economic opportunities. As from a societal viewpoint, they have improved global connectivity but at the same time introduced ethical problems about our privacy, misinformation, and mental health problems. 

## First Viewpoint analysis:  
##### Business: The rise of social media in the business world has evolved from a simple communication tool to a proven catalyst for increasing growth, marketing, sales and improved customer relationships. Before social media marketing for a business was mainly through offline avenues such as tv radio and outdoor advertising such as billboards and signs. Marketing before social media required more physical resources that has since digitalized such as loyalty cards which are now all scannable QR codes that you can scan from your phone instead of carrying physical cards before social media marketing digitalized the way businesses kept track of a customer's visiting frequency without using paper which has its environmental benefits. The adoption of digital loyalty cards meant processing users' data could be done quicker, which also helped when companies wanted to make focus groups and surveys as they were able to retrieve data quicker due to computational power.  Since the late 2000's social media usage in business rose with the growth of Facebook and X formerly known as twitter as smartphones were getting faster internet speeds which made accessing these platforms anywhere easier while these platforms launched ad programs that were precise targeted ads based off users' data. With the rise of social media influencers paved a new way for businesses to advertise to new crowds of people by sponsored ads on the social media platforms of influencers in the form of free products being sent to an influencer and shown on their platforms or simply just the influencer dedicating part of the video to talk about a business and their products/services for an agreed upon fee by the influencer and the business. This form of advertising is commonly used now as the influencers' fan base shows social proof of a product being used which encourages the influencers' followers to use the same product which is extremely affective for business sales. 

##### Society: The evolution of social media has positively impacted society by revolutionizing the way people communicate with each other while providing a platform for creativity, self-expression and for giving a voice to marginalized communities and helping to spread awareness of movements for change. It has also became a catalyst for the speed in which individuals can share knowledge to a large group of people or for faster collaboration and communication on projects. Communication has been made easier and instantaneous between people no matter of the geographical location due to apps like Facebook and Instagram where people maintain their relationships with friends and family on those platforms among many others that are now available. With these new platforms providing a new means of communication, it also provides more diversity as these apps are downloaded globally, which allows real-time interaction between people from diverse cultural backgrounds and areas. The rise of social media has also made education free and globally accessible. With platforms like YouTube being the first free video sharing platform to gain global popularity, educational videos spanning a large range of topics are available from educators worldwide free of charge which also helps remote learning for people who can't attend education in person.
## Second Viewpoint analysis: 
##### Business: Businesses must be able to upload regular content in order to give their advertisements the best chance of getting picked up by social media platforms algorithms to stay relevant and to maximize the amount of people who will see what they post as inactive accounts across most social media platforms do not push content from inactive accounts as heavily as they do with regularly active accounts. Maintaining an active social media account for a business is also very draining on resources as it requires a lot of time to be invested into the social media presence and qualified staff to run the social media pages. The return on investment can also be difficult to track as the resources invested into a social media account to get likes and shares is not a guaranteed way to get more sales for a business. Social media also gives equal access to negative reviews of a business which can spread like wildfire across the internet and potentially damage a business's reputation. Bad reviews are now easier to do as most platforms offer a level of anonymity to someone writing a positive or negative review which makes reviewing a business publicly more guiltfree. 
##### Society: The rise of social media had had several negative effects on society, including but not limited to the spreading of misinformation at high speeds and reaching more people quicker than ever, negatively impacting mental health in the likes of increased anxiety and depression and a lack of privacy. Social media can also cause addiction to social media platforms, which in turn can cause reduced productivity in professional and educational settings. The spreading of false or misleading information on social media can spread quickly without proper fact checking can lead to confusion and false narratives which can cause real world harm. Increased use of social media is regularly linked to higher rates of anxiety, depression, and loneliness. Some of these issues can be caused by seeing the unrealistic versions of the lives of other people or unrealistic beauty standards that are commonly seen all over any social media platform. Due to the design of how social media is consumed, it is very easy to get distracted by the urge to check notifications or refresh a feed on a platform instead of doing important tasks in the workplace or school. This can be seen in recent years as schools in Ireland were able to avail to a government funded scheme to get phone pouches or lock boxes for students to put their phones in during the school day. This was to reduce the distractions caused by social media on students' phones while promoting social interaction to help create a more academically focused environment. Privacy concerns are also a threat which has risen with the rise of social media as the business model of most platforms relies on the collection of users' data which raises privacy and security concerns on how the data is managed.
