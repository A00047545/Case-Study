## First Viewpoint analysis:  
##### Business: The rise of social media in the business world has evolved from a simple communication tool to a proven catalyst for increasing growth, marketing, sales and improved customer relationships. Before social media marketing for a business was mainly through offline avenues such as tv radio and outdoor advertising such as billboards and signs. Marketing before social media required more physical resources that has since digitalized such as loyalty cards which are now all scannable QR codes that you can scan from your phone instead of carrying physical cards before social media marketing digitalized the way businesses kept track of a customer's visiting frequency without using paper which has its environmental benefits. The adoption of digital loyalty cards meant processing users' data could be done quicker, which also helped when companies wanted to make focus groups and surveys as they were able to retrieve data quicker due to computational power.  Since the late 2000's social media usage in business rose with the growth of Facebook and X formerly known as twitter as smartphones were getting faster internet speeds which made accessing these platforms anywhere easier while these platforms launched ad programs that were precise targeted ads based off users' data. With the rise of social media influencers paved a new way for businesses to advertise to new crowds of people by sponsored ads on the social media platforms of influencers in the form of free products being sent to an influencer and shown on their platforms or simply just the influencer dedicating part of the video to talk about a business and their products/services for an agreed upon fee by the influencer and the business. This form of advertising is commonly used now as the influencers' fan base shows social proof of a product being used which encourages the influencers' followers to use the same product which is extremely affective for business sales. 

##### Society: The evolution of social media has positively impacted society by revolutionizing the way people communicate with each other while providing a platform for creativity, self-expression and for giving a voice to marginalized communities and helping to spread awareness of movements for change. It has also became a catalyst for the speed in which individuals can share knowledge to a large group of people or for faster collaboration and communication on projects. Communication has been made easier and instantaneous between people no matter of the geographical location due to apps like Facebook and Instagram where people maintain their relationships with friends and family on those platforms among many others that are now available. With these new platforms providing a new means of communication, it also provides more diversity as these apps are downloaded globally, which allows real-time interaction between people from diverse cultural backgrounds and areas. The rise of social media has also made education free and globally accessible. With platforms like YouTube being the first free video sharing platform to gain global popularity, educational videos spanning a large range of topics are available from educators worldwide free of charge which also helps remote learning for people who can't attend education in person.
## Second Viewpoint analysis: 
##### Business: Businesses must be able to upload regular content in order to give their advertisements the best chance of getting picked up by social media platforms algorithms to stay relevant and to maximize the amount of people who will see what they post as inactive accounts across most social media platforms do not push content from inactive accounts as heavily as they do with regularly active accounts. Maintaining an active social media account for a business is also very draining on resources as it requires a lot of time to be invested into the social media presence and qualified staff to run the social media pages. The return on investment can also be difficult to track as the resources invested into a social media account to get likes and shares is not a guaranteed way to get more sales for a business. Social media also gives equal access to negative reviews of a business which can spread like wildfire across the internet and potentially damage a business's reputation. Bad reviews are now easier to do as most platforms offer a level of anonymity to someone writing a positive or negative review which makes reviewing a business publicly more guiltfree. 
##### The rise of social media had had several negative effects on society, including but not limited to the spreading of misinformation at high speeds and reaching more people quicker than ever, negatively impacting mental health in the likes of increased anxiety and depression and a lack of privacy. Social media can also cause addiction to social media platforms, which in turn can cause reduced productivity in professional and educational settings. The spreading of false or misleading information on social media can spread quickly without proper fact checking can lead to confusion and false narratives which can cause real world harm. Increased use of social media is regularly linked to higher rates of anxiety, depression, and loneliness. Some of these issues can be caused by seeing the unrealistic versions of the lives of other people or unrealistic beauty standards that are commonly seen all over any social media platform. Due to the design of how social media is consumed, it is very easy to get distracted by the urge to check notifications or refresh a feed on a platform instead of doing important tasks in the workplace or school. This can be seen in recent years as schools in Ireland were able to avail to a government funded scheme to get phone pouches or lock boxes for students to put their phones in during the school day. This was to reduce the distractions caused by social media on students' phones while promoting social interaction to help create a more academically focused environment. Privacy concerns are also a threat which has risen with the rise of social media as the business model of most platforms relies on the collection of users' data which raises privacy and security concerns on how the data is managed.
