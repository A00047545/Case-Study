# Introduction - Caio's work
This case study explains one of the most influential forces of modern era, consumer technology refers to all technology designed for everyday use, it is accessible to everyone and it’s the main goal is to be affordable and as convenient as possible for all consumers.

The main topics in this case study will be the historical development, which will include a timeline and explanations on how consumer technology first came to be. first and second viewpoint analysis, and ethical reflections & improvements.

Consumer technology has evolved quite a lot since its beginning over a century ago, changing our ways of living, specially changing the way we communicate. This evolution indicates the development of tools and devices to make them more accessible, convenient, easy to use and affordable for most people, it has changed from relying on mechanical tools and devices to replying mostly on digital systems such as smartphones, microwaves, laptops and such.
