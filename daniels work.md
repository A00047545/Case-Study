# Historical Evolution: From Origins to the Present
a. Evolution of Social Media Platforms 

Social media began in the early 2000s, with MySpace being the first media site to reach 1 million users per month, and it reached its peak monthly users around 2004. MySpace was a simple platform that allowed users to build personalized profiles, upload music and backgrounds, and connect with friends. We  can safely say that it paved the way for the next generations of platforms such as Facebook and instagram.  These new platforms allow for better privacy settings, offered a cleaner design and faster communication between servers to allow and all in general faster app. As apps platforms developed, some have shifted toward more of a short quick pace video format. Apps such as Vine, TikTok, and instagram are a good example, because of this, people got addictive due to the dopamine they gave us.

b. Development of Search Engines 

 
 
The early stages of search engines were very  simple, with Yahoo (founded in 1994) relying on manual listing, which had users manually search and browse pre-made categories. In 1988, Google was launched, which revolutionized web browsing with its new PageRank  algorithm , which is still used today. This algorithm is based on ranking websites based on relevance and links. This allowed Google to be faster, more user friendly,  and quickly gain users, beating their competitors. In todays world, the search engines were introduced to the  so called “AI-powerd era” with tools like chat-gpt, Google Gemini, and Microsoft Copoilot. This not only helped with the speed and the accuracy of the users search but also tries to understand the users intent and provide an answer.

c. Transformation of Streaming Services 
 
 

The early stages of streaming services started in the late 1990s and early  2000s starting out as physical media such as VHS tapes, DVDs, and CDs. The early 2000s saw the rise of streaming services such as Netflix which went from a DVD-by-mail service into an online powerhouse in 2007, offering downloadable films and tv shows on demand. For music, streaming services like, sound cloud and  spotify quickly rose in popularity allowing for music to be streamed legally instead of downloading them. By 2010s, streaming services had become mainstream with companies such as Disney+, Amazone Prime Video, and Hulu capitalized on the growth for the demand for streaming content.
